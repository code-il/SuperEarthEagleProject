SPHEREx Atlas & Explorer - Sources
===================================

Every value in the Atlas and Explorer comes from the public sources below, or is
computed from them. None of them needs an account or API key.


DATA SOURCES
------------

1. NASA SPHEREx - Level 2 spectral images and all-sky HiPS (DR1, QR3), via NASA/IPAC IRSA
   Used in:  Atlas and Explorer
   Used for: time-lapse frames, spectra, measured classes, object-card images
   Terms:    Public NASA data. Acknowledge: "This publication makes use of data from
             SPHEREx, a NASA mission managed by JPL/Caltech, obtained from IRSA."
   Links:    https://www.jpl.nasa.gov/missions/spherex/
             https://irsa.ipac.caltech.edu/Missions/spherex.html

2. Gaia DR3 (ESA/Gaia/DPAC)
   Used in:  Atlas and Explorer
   Used for: Atlas positions and distances; Explorer overlays and motion checks
   Terms:    CC BY-SA 3.0 IGO. Cite Gaia Collaboration 2016, 2023.
   Link:     https://www.cosmos.esa.int/web/gaia/dr3

3. SIMBAD (CDS, Strasbourg)
   Used in:  Atlas
   Used for: names, aliases, object types and spectral types
   Terms:    Free for scientific use. Cite Wenger et al. 2000.
   Link:     https://simbad.cds.unistra.fr/

4. NASA Exoplanet Archive (pscomppars table)
   Used in:  Atlas
   Used for: planet orbits, masses and host stars
   Terms:    Public. Acknowledge the NASA Exoplanet Archive, operated by Caltech under
             contract with NASA.
   Link:     https://exoplanetarchive.ipac.caltech.edu/

5. JPL Horizons
   Used in:  Atlas
   Used for: Solar System positions used to check the N-body orbit engine
   Terms:    Public (JPL Solar System Dynamics).
   Link:     https://ssd.jpl.nasa.gov/horizons/

6. Kopparapu et al. 2014, ApJL 787, L29
   Used in:  Atlas
   Used for: habitable-zone edges
   Terms:    Published paper.

7. CatWISE2020 (via IRSA)
   Used in:  Explorer
   Used for: catalogue overlays, infrared proper motions
   Terms:    Public. Cite Marocco et al. 2021.
   Link:     https://irsa.ipac.caltech.edu/data/WISE/CatWISE/overview.html

8. CDS Sesame
   Used in:  Atlas and Explorer
   Used for: object names to coordinates
   Terms:    Free service of CDS.
   Link:     https://cds.unistra.fr/cgi-bin/Sesame

9. IMCCE SkyBoT
   Used in:  Explorer
   Used for: known asteroids in the field on each date
   Terms:    Free service of IMCCE.
   Link:     https://ssp.imcce.fr/webservices/skybot/

10. Aladin Lite (CDS), with AllWISE, 2MASS and DSS2 surveys
   Used in:  Explorer
   Used for: sky context panel
   Terms:    GPL-3.0.
   Link:     https://aladin.cds.unistra.fr/AladinLite/
